Wiseguard Security Services History

Wiseguard Security Services is headquartered in Orange Grove, Johannesburg, Gauteng, and provides physical security, armed response, electronic security, investigations, and VIP protection services to residential, commercial, and industrial clients. The company operates from 151 Louis Botha Avenue, Orange Grove.

Founding and Early Years

  • 1994 appears on several business directories as the year the company was established.
  • 1997 is listed on the company's LinkedIn profile as the founding year.

    • The company was founded during a significant period in South Africa's history. Following the country's transition to democracy in 1994, the private security industry expanded rapidly due to increased demand for private protection from businesses, residential estates, shopping centres, and industrial facilities. Wiseguard entered the market during this period of growth.

    Growth of the Company

    Wiseguard Security Services has grown to become a reputable security company in South Africa, known for its commitment to providing high-quality security services. The company has expanded its offerings to include a wide range of security solutions, catering to the diverse needs of its clients.

    • Physical guarding
    • Armed response
    • Asset protection
    • VIP protection
    • Event security
    • Escort services
    • Canine (dog) handling
    • Crime prevention
    • Investigations
    • CCTV installation
    • Alarm systems
    • Access control
    • Guard monitoring systems
    • Control room operations
    • Residential and industrial security solutions

      • This diversification reflects a broader trend within South Africa's security industry, where companies increasingly combine manpower with electronic security technologies.
